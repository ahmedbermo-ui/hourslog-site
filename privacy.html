Hours Log — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Hours Log is a simple weekly hours log. It works on your device. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics.

What we collect

Nothing. The clients and hours you enter are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions.

Tracking

Hours Log does not track you, does not take screenshots and does not monitor activity.

Children

Hours Log is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, use the contact email shown on the Hours Log listing in Google Play.
